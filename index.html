FAQ常见问题

Good questions你可能想问

Is Lumo really free?Lumo 真的免费吗? +

Yes — free forever and open source. No credit card, no paywalled features.

是的 —— 永久免费,而且开源。无需信用卡,没有付费墙功能。

Do I need to install anything?需要安装什么吗? +

No. Lumo runs in your browser and can be added to your home screen as a PWA for an app-like experience with offline support.

不需要。Lumo 在浏览器里就能用,也可作为 PWA 添加到主屏,获得接近原生、支持离线的体验。

How does the AI planning work?AI 规划是怎么工作的? +

Describe your day or drop in your tasks; Lumo drafts a time-boxed, priority-aware plan you can adjust before committing.

描述你的一天或丢进任务,Lumo 会按时间预算和优先级排出计划,确认前你可以随手调整。

Is my data synced and private?数据会同步吗?私密吗? +

Your tasks sync across your devices through your Lumo account — stored on our servers, used only to run your account, with no third-party ad trackers. It's open source, so you can inspect exactly how your data is handled.

任务通过你的 Lumo 账号在各设备间同步 —— 数据存放在我们的服务器上、仅用于运行你的账号,不含第三方广告追踪。项目开源,你可以清楚地看到数据是怎么被处理的。

English and Chinese both supported?中英文都支持吗? +

Fully bilingual — switch the whole app (and this site) between English and 中文 anytime.

完整双语 —— 整个应用(以及本站)随时可在 English 与中文之间切换。
