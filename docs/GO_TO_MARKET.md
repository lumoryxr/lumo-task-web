# Lumo — Go-to-Market Plan (Free Beta)

Audience focus (from the product owner): **overseas individual productivity users**,
**domestic (China) individual users**, and the **developer / tech community**. Team
and enterprise are out of scope for now (Lumo is single-user multi-tenant; shared
workspaces don't exist yet).

Lumo's three sharpest hooks:
1. **The virtual pet + gamified focus** — evolving pet, XP/levels, celebration moments,
   shareable "productivity wrapped" cards. This is the viral, screenshot-friendly angle.
2. **Eisenhower Matrix + Pomodoro + AI classification** — a credible, opinionated
   productivity system, not just another to-do list.
3. **"100% AI-coded"** — every commit and PR generated by Claude via an autonomous
   engineering loop. A genuinely novel story for the developer audience.

> Do the P0 items in `LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md` first. Launching to a wave of traffic
> without password reset, error tracking, or backups turns a good launch into churn.

---

## 1. Overseas individual users (English)

**Channels**
- **Product Hunt** — the anchor launch. Lead with a GIF of the pet evolving + the
  wrapped card. Prepare assets a week ahead; line up hunters/supporters; launch Tue–Thu
  ~12:01am PT. Have the team answer every comment on launch day.
- **X/Twitter (build-in-public)** — the "100% AI-coded productivity app" narrative is
  tailor-made here. Post the autonomous-loop story, before/afters, and pet clips.
- **Reddit** — r/productivity, r/getdisciplined, r/pomodoro. Lead with value/story,
  not a bare link (respect each sub's self-promo rules).
- **Newsletters/directories** — productivity newsletters, AlternativeTo, SaaS
  directories, "tools I use" roundups.

**Prep**
- English landing page polished; add OG/Twitter cards so shared links preview well.
- A 60–90s demo video (capture → classify → focus → pet reward → wrapped card).

## 2. Domestic individual users (China, 中文)

**Channels**
- **小红书 (RED)** — the pet + matrix visuals are ideal for this platform. Short
  "我的效率仪式" posts with the wrapped card and pet screenshots.
- **少数派 (sspai)** — a launch feature/review carries real weight with this audience;
  pitch the editorial angle (opinionated system + AI + gamification).
- **即刻, V2EX, 微信公众号, B站** — build-in-public updates; a B站 demo walkthrough.

**Prep**
- Chinese landing page is already bilingual — make sure the pricing/legal copy is
  consistent with the free-beta reality.
- Confirm the hosted backend is reachable and fast from mainland China (test latency;
  consider region/CDN). This is often the make-or-break for CN adoption.

## 3. Developer / tech community

**Channels**
- **Hacker News (Show HN)** — lead with the **"100% AI-coded, humans forbidden from
  editing code"** angle + the autonomous 2-hour engineering loop. This is the strongest
  HN hook; the productivity app is the artifact that proves it.
- **GitHub** — polish the README (hero GIF, live demo link, architecture); the
  contract-first design, test pyramid, and DFX/security suites are a credible flex.
- **掘金 / dev.to / 开发者播客** — long-form technical writeups: contract-first
  monorepo, the autonomous loop, how the AI-only workflow actually holds quality.

**Prep**
- A written "how this was built" post is the center of gravity for this audience.
- Make the live demo trivially reachable and resilient (see backup/monitoring P0/P1).

---

## Cross-cutting

- **Instrument the funnel first.** Before driving traffic, wire analytics + error
  tracking (P1) so you can see register → activate (first task/first focus) → retain,
  and catch launch-day breakage immediately.
- **Feedback loop.** Add a visible "report a bug / request a feature" path and a
  monitored inbox; beta users who feel heard become advocates.
- **Sequencing.** Soft-launch to a small group → fix the obvious → then the coordinated
  PH/HN/RED push on the same week → iterate publicly on feedback.
- **Assets to prepare once, reuse everywhere:** the demo video, the pet-evolution GIF,
  the wrapped card, 5–6 screenshots (light+dark), and the "how it was built" post.
- **Honesty in positioning.** Keep the "free beta" framing explicit and reconcile the
  local-first "no tracking" marketing line with the hosted account product — trust is
  cheap to keep and expensive to rebuild.
