Privacy-First
隐私优先

Local-first —
you choose where your data lives

本地优先 —
数据存在哪由你决定

On desktop, everything stays on your device — like Obsidian, you pick the path and own it completely. A hosted account is optional, for when you want cloud sync across devices.

桌面版所有数据留在你的设备上 — 像 Obsidian 一样，你选择路径、完全拥有它。需要跨设备时，再用可选的云端账号版。

📁
Custom storage path
自定义存储路径
Store your database anywhere — local disk, Dropbox, iCloud, or a NAS.
数据库可存放在任意位置 — 本地磁盘、Dropbox、iCloud 或 NAS。
🔌
Desktop works fully offline
桌面版完全离线
The desktop app needs no internet — it works perfectly on planes, submarines, and anywhere in between. (The web app needs a connection.)
桌面应用无需联网 — 飞机、地下室、任何地方都能完美运行。（Web 版需要联网。）
🔐
No third-party ad tracking
无第三方广告追踪
No ad trackers, ever. In local desktop mode nothing is sent to our servers; with a hosted account your data is encrypted and used only to run your account.
永不接入广告追踪。桌面本地模式下数据不发往我们的服务器；使用云端账号时，数据加密存储、仅用于运行你的账号。
